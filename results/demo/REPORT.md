MAE and RMSE measure percentage-point error. Lower is better. Direction accuracy compares positive, negative, and exactly zero signs; higher is better. A zero forecast is neutral and is correct only when the actual return is exactly zero.

These are descriptive scores. Shared dates, indexes, and overlapping horizons make observations dependent; scores alone do not establish statistical significance. The results do not establish trading profitability.
